# Wesh Star IA — Free Trial 10s / 2 Uses Lifetime

- Free trial duration: **10 seconds per generation**
- Maximum: **2 successful/reserved trial generations per account, lifetime**
- After the two lifetime trials, a paid quota is required.
- The lifetime counter is enforced atomically in PostgreSQL (`consume_free_trial`).
- Failed/duplicate/concurrency-rejected jobs refund the reserved trial automatically.
- Paid quota packs, Admin quota management, Moderator and Autopilot functionality are preserved.
