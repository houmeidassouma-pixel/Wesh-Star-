# Wesh Star IA — Continuous Minute Credit Packs Update

- Pricing UI now presents customer credit packs from **1 minute through 30 minutes**.
- Each minute pack has its own configurable EUR price.
- **60 credits = 1 minute**.
- Free trial remains exactly **2 lifetime generations of 10 seconds each**.
- Admin can edit, add, remove, enable or disable each 1–60 minute credit pack.
- This one-time credit-pack grid is separate from monthly subscription quotas.
- Autopilot keeps its own platform-specific duration options and is not reduced to the customer credit-pack range.
